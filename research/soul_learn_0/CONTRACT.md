software-only deterministic environment with a bounded hypothesis language,
meaningful examples, held-out cases and a genuine counterexample. Measure
memorization, generalization, revision and retention separately. Maintain

Registered test: learner is given only a finite general hypothesis language,
not the environment's target. Train on observations, make predictions before
feedback, present one counterexample, and measure held-out predictions and
serialized retention afterward. A memorization-only baseline receives identical
training. Preserve the bounded language, stimuli, expected results, controls,
source hashes, outputs and limitations. Fixed thresholds: BOUNDED PASS requires
correct initial example recall, at least one correct held-out prediction, actual
hypothesis rejection and change after the counterexample, a correct unseen
prediction after revision and exact retained revision after serialization, with
explicit unknown/unresolved/rejected tests and deterministic reproduction.
Otherwise report PARTIAL or FAIL; no result proves general intelligence. No
production integration, publication, network, external model or hardware work.
