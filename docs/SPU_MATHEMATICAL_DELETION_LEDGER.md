# SPU Mathematical Deletion Ledger (provisional, 2026-10-23)

Purpose: record which mathematical mechanisms the SPU needs **not** introduce or could unify, on evidence from three exploratory investigations. Provisional; no experiments run for this ledger; no code, ISA, RTL, simulator or QCG-0 change.
**No existing implementation is deleted or proposed for deletion here.** "DO NOT INTRODUCE" means a proposed new mechanism is not justified by the evidence; it is not a removal.
Evidence level: **M** = exact symbolic/arithmetic identity over the stated finite range; **S** = software-measured operation counts (proxies, not hardware cost); **H** = hardware-demonstrated (none of the entries below).

## Three most credible complexity reductions
1. **One recurrence/oracle family** (rows 1–3, 11): S_n, T_n, Lucas V_n, Pell powers and traces from one recurrence in the test/oracle layer. *Assumptions to verify before any removal:* bit-exact agreement with the current oracles and tables over their full operating ranges (including 16-bit Pell octave limits); explicit Q=+1 vs Q=−1 handling; whether any current table is *not* a recurrence output; resource and latency comparison for any shared datapath (none measured).
2. **One relation oracle for spread/twist/velocity/quadrea** (rows 10, 12): a single polynomial family replacing per-feature fixtures. *Assumptions:* field of definition per use (A31 null vectors, rows 15), singular points (V=1, perpendicular), root-branch selection for the two-valued cases, agreement with the VM's current `spread()` semantics (known inconsistency) before touching it.
3. **Structural-law certificates instead of searches** (rows 13, 14, 8): reuse the PH low-degree form and the determinant rule rather than rediscovering sign per curve. *Assumptions:* more than 16 nontrivial PH samples and other distributions; generalized-PH sign factor h(t); invalidation rules when the PH form is lost (89% of affine steps); software-only until a hardware need is shown.

## Boundaries
- Calculus stays: the §19 negative result is about a generic interval *positivity certificate*, not Taylor algebra (jets, tangents, multiplicity — row 6).
- Distinctions used (not hardware units): composable state (turn/Pell pair/PH generator), derived observables (spread, quadrance, speed, curvature sign), structural laws (identities above), decision procedures (Sturm).
- Every row is mathematical or software-measured; none is hardware-demonstrated, qualified, or an accepted architectural decision.
