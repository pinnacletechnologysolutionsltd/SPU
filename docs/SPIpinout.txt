  For the female DE-15 socket, wire these nine contacts:

  - Pin 1: Red
  - Pin 2: Green
  - Pin 3: Blue
  - Pin 6: Red return/GND
  - Pin 7: Green return/GND
  - Pin 8: Blue return/GND
  - Pin 10: Sync return/GND
  - Pin 13: HSync
  - Pin 14: VSync

  Use separate conductors for returns 6, 7, and 8, joining them to board ground at the adapter. The older XDC
  comment also permits grounding DE-15 pin 5, but the newer harness specification uses the nine contacts
  above; pin 5 is not required.

  Important:

  - Do not connect DE-15 pin 9: it carries +5 V from the display side.
  - Leave DDC pins 12 and 15 disconnected.
  - Leave ID pins 4 and 11 disconnected.
  - Do not connect J10 pins 6 or 12; those are 3.3 V.
  - J10 pins 7, 8, and 9 are unused by VGA.
  - The three 270 Ω resistors are required on RGB only. Sync is direct.
  - VGA cable wire colours are not standardized—identify every conductor by continuity.
  - DE-15 numbering is mirrored between the mating face and solder-cup rear. Follow the socket’s stamped
    numbers and verify with continuity before powering anything.
