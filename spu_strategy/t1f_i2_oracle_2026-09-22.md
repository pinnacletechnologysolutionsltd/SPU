The fixture is `A5` followed by four unchanged 252-bit records, encoded in
record order 0 through 3. All four records use the same triangle coverage:

```text
edge 0:  0*x + 60*y - 600 >= 0
edge 1: -80*x - 30*y + 5900 >= 0
edge 2: 80*x - 30*y - 500 >= 0
```

The depth fields are deliberately chosen as four affine planes under the
existing RTL depth convention (`edge_i * z_i`):

| Record | RGB | z0 | z1 | z2 |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | red `F00` | 110 | 0 | 60 |
| 1 | green `0F0` | 110 | 60 | 0 |
| 2 | blue `00F` | 10 | 60 | 120 |
| 3 | yellow `FF0` | 10 | 120 | 60 |

At the representative pixels below, all four primitives are covered. The
listed depths are the exact affine values before the implementation's fixed-
point output representation; the winner is the smallest value.

|---:|---:|---|---:|---|
| 5 | 5 | background | none | `000` |

The focused top-level simulation also permutes the same geometric
primitive/depth/colour tuples among record slots. All four unequal-depth
winner colours remain unchanged. The equal-depth permutation case is excluded
from that invariant and is covered separately by the lower-record tie result.
